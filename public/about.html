Features

A complete audio workspace for your table

Everything you need to prepare, organize, and perform the soundtrack of your game.

🎚️

Layered sound scenes

Mix multiple music, ambience, voice, and effect tracks inside a single playable scene.

🌐

Synchronized playback

Broadcast scenes to everyone in the room or keep playback private to the game master.

🔊

Precise volume control

Balance master, scene, and individual track volume while every player controls local listening level.

🔁

Natural repetition

Configure loops, randomized delays, fade in, and fade out for organic ambient sound.

🗂️

Organized libraries

Group scenes into colored folders and give folders and scenes expressive emoji icons.

💾

Portable backups

Import and export complete `.aforge` libraries, including compatible Djinni collections.

Designed for play

Your soundtrack stays close, not in the way

Ambient Forge lives inside Owlbear Rodeo and keeps audio running even after its popover is closed.

  • Use direct MP3, OGG, WAV, and YouTube sources.
  • Pause and resume a scene without starting it over.
  • See what is playing and control everything from one compact panel.
  • Save your library automatically in the browser.
Quick start

From silence to atmosphere in three steps

Add the extension

Copy the install link below and add it from your Owlbear Rodeo profile.

Create a sound scene

Make a folder, add a scene, then layer one or more audio sources.

Set the mood

Choose Everyone or Only me, enable audio once, and press play.

Browsers require one user interaction before reliable audio playback. Each listener should press Enable audio once after joining the room.
Install Ambient Forge

Bring your world to life

In your Owlbear Rodeo profile, choose Add Extension and paste the manifest URL below. After the store listing is approved, you will also be able to install Ambient Forge directly from the Owlbear Extension Store.

https://ambient-forge.marevo.workers.dev/manifest.json
